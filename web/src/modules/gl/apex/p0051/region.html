How a journal moves

From the first line to the account balances, in four steps.

Where to startMenu → Journals → Journal entry, or the New journal button.
Where each step is doneInside the journal, with the button of its step. Approvers find their journals in Inbox: waiting approval, posters in Inbox: ready to post.
Journals from other modulesMortgage, leasing, treasury ... arrive already submitted and follow steps 3 and 4.
